Privacy Policy

Ink Swing · Effective October 12, 2026 · Developer: Tony Nguyen (GitHub: ntony7898)

Ink Swing is a single-player game. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no chat, and we do not run any servers that collect your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Best scores, settings (sound on/off, theme, mode) and whether you bought “Remove Ads” are saved locally on your device only. Deleting the app (or clearing site data on the web) removes them.
  • Score posters you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them.

Game Center (iOS app)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores are submitted to Ink Swing leaderboards. Apple processes this under your Game Center settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. We only see what Apple shows on public leaderboards (your Game Center nickname and score).

In-app purchase (iOS app)

The optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the app only checks with Apple whether the purchase exists so it can be restored.

Advertising (iOS app)

The app shows an ad only when you choose to watch one (for example, to continue a run). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device and usage data, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and prevent fraud. On iOS you are asked for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, non-personalised ads are shown. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying “Remove Ads” stops ads entirely.

Web version

The website version (ntony7898.github.io/ink-swing) contains no ads, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data (such as IP address) as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Children

Ink Swing is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests can be limited to non-personalised ads.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ntony7898/ink-swing/issues.
